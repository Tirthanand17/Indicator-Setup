NIFTY Automatic Five-Indicator Alerts v0.2

Automatic screen-reading beta, not an execution bot. Reads only the displayed NIFTY futures chart legend within Kite. No manual indicator input, no trading API, no payment, and no orders. Near-close chart samples are not exchange-confirmed closed candles; every alert means REVIEW, not buy immediately. Actual option quotes, IV and your holdings are not accessible from a futures-only chart.

3 · Paper-signal evaluation at 5-minute boundaries

No near-close sample yet.

Alerts require a snapshot from the last 3.5 seconds before the next bar, observation of the next bar shortly after the boundary, four consecutive near-close bars, ADX≥25 and rising, aligned VWAP/EMA/DMI, and a break of the recent 3-bar price range. Signals also use a 15-minute trend proxy from 5-minute price history; this is not an independent 15-minute chart calculation.

4 · Virtual exit alert tracker (NOT Kite holdings)

After a candidate CE/PE alert, the system automatically tracks a virtual underlying futures stop/target/reversal and can notify EXIT CE or EXIT PE. It does not know whether you bought an option, what premium you paid, or the real option BID. It cannot close your actual position.
